What this prototype models

Multi-hop forwarding, finite queues, lossy links, retries, battery depletion, local neighbor advertisements, and route adaptation.

What the results mean

Energy is illustrative, not measured in joules. Links are independent; RF collisions and MAC contention are not modeled. Initial BFS levels stay fixed. This is an algorithm prototype, not a full RPL implementation.

PAMR · Deterministic seeded experiments · No guaranteed performance improvement · Read MODEL.md and VIVA_GUIDE.md before presenting.
